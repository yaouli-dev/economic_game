# economic_game
an interactive web-based economic simulation in which players manage a small business and make decisions about pricing, wages, sourcing, marketing, and competition. Instead of having “correct” answers, every decision creates trade-offs between profit, reputation, and the welfare of customers, workers, the community, and the environment. A continuous market model allows earlier decisions to influence later outcomes. At the end, players receive an report showing the consequences of their decisions and can replay with a different objective to explore how different goals change what counts as a “rational” choice.
